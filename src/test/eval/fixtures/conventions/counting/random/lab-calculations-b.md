# Physics Lab Preparation — Worked Calculations (Set B)
Each problem gives data read from lab instruments. Solutions by the lab instructors.

## Problem 1
A lamp is connected to a 12.4 V supply and the ammeter shows 0.83 A. What power does the lamp draw?

**Solution.** The electric power is the product of voltage and current:
$$P = VI = 12.4\ \text{V} \cdot 0.83\ \text{A} = 10.29\ \text{W}.$$
The label on the lamp lists a power close to this value.

## Problem 2
A trolley's speed, read from the sensor, rises by 5.6 m/s in 2.3 s. Find its average acceleration.

**Solution.** Average acceleration is the change in speed over the time interval:
$$\bar a = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = \frac{5.6\ \text{m/s}}{2.3\ \text{s}} = 2.43\ \text{m/s}^2.$$
We assume the acceleration is constant over the interval.

## Problem 3
A solution contains 3.7 g of salt in 0.45 L of water, both measured in the lab. Find the concentration in g/L.

**Solution.** Concentration is the mass of the solute per unit volume of solution:
$$c = \frac{3.7\ \text{g}}{0.45\ \text{L}} = 8.2222222\ \text{g/L}.$$
The volume of the salt itself is ignored, as it is small.

## Problem 4
A 0.75 kg cart rolls at a speed of 3.3 m/s, as measured by the motion sensor. What is its kinetic energy?

**Solution.** The kinetic energy is $E_k = \frac12 m v^2$:
$$E_k = \frac12 \cdot 0.75\ \text{kg} \cdot (3.3\ \text{m/s})^2 = 4.08375\ \text{J}.$$
The energy is later compared with the work done by the pushing force.
