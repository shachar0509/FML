# Physics Lab Preparation — Worked Calculations (Set A)
Each problem gives data read from lab instruments. Solutions by the lab instructors.

## Problem 1
In the track experiment a cart travels 2.35 m in 1.6 s. Find its average speed.

**Solution.** Average speed is distance over time:
$$\bar v = \frac{2.35\ \text{m}}{1.6\ \text{s}} = 1.47\ \text{m/s}.$$
The cart moves at a roughly steady pace, so this single number describes the run.

## Problem 2
A resistor is connected to a supply. The voltmeter reads 4.7 V and the ammeter reads 0.32 A. Find the resistance.

**Solution.** By Ohm's law, $R = V/I$:
$$R = \frac{4.7\ \text{V}}{0.32\ \text{A}} = 14.6875\ \Omega.$$
The same value is obtained from the slope of the V-I line in the lab report.

## Problem 3
A metal sample has a measured mass of 27.3 g and a measured volume of 10.1 cm³. Find its density.

**Solution.** Density is mass per unit volume:
$$\rho = \frac{27.3\ \text{g}}{10.1\ \text{cm}^3} = 2.70\ \text{g/cm}^3.$$
The sample is then identified from the density table.

## Problem 4
A pendulum is timed: 15 full oscillations take 27.8 s. Find the period.

**Solution.** One oscillation takes the total time divided by the number of oscillations:
$$T = \frac{27.8\ \text{s}}{15} = 1.8533333\ \text{s}.$$
Timing fifteen swings keeps the reaction time of the person with the stopwatch from dominating the result.
