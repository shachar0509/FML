# Applied Mathematics — Grading Rubric for the Midterm (prepared by the lecturer)

## Scoring of final answers
Each question is graded out of 15 points: 10 for the method, 3 for the correct final answer, 2 for presentation.

Final answers must be given in exact form: fractions, radicals, $\pi$, $e$ and logarithms are left as they are. A decimal approximation written as the final answer where an exact form exists costs 3 points (the whole mark for the final answer).

The only exception is a question that explicitly asks you to estimate or to approximate; there a decimal is expected and an exact form is not required.

Do not round intermediate results.
