# Torts and Contracts — Grading Rubric for Problem Questions
Issued by the lecturer to the examiners. Applies to every problem question (the 25-30 point questions) in the 2025 exams.

## Allocation of marks (per 100 points of a problem question)

| Component | Points |
|---|---|
| Identifying the legal issues raised by the facts | 20 |
| Stating the governing rule accurately | 25 |
| Applying the rule to the specific facts, including the counter-arguments | 40 |
| Reaching a conclusion that follows from the analysis | 15 |

## Specific deductions

- Statutory citation: every statute you rely on must be cited with its section number (-5 per missing section). Naming the statute alone is not enough. A section number that is wrong is treated as missing. A statute that is only mentioned in passing, where the answer does not rest on it, carries no deduction.
- Facts invented or ignored: -5 for each material fact ignored, -5 for each fact invented.
- A rule stated without any application to the facts: the application component is capped at 15.
- An answer that argues one side only, with no discussion of the other party's best argument: -10.
- Citing a case by name without stating what it decided: -3 each.

## Notes for examiners

Give credit for a sound argument even if the examinee reaches a different conclusion from the model solution. A conclusion that contradicts the examinee's own analysis loses the whole of the conclusion component.

Definition questions (for example, "define consideration") are marked on accuracy and on the quality of the explanation; the deductions for statutory citation do not apply where the answer does not depend on a statute.
